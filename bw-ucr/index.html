A Business Wallet lets an organisation — a "legal person" — hold, manage and present Verifiable Credentials (VCs) about itself: its legal existence, its authorisations and certifications, and the mandates it grants to the natural persons who act on its behalf. Such wallets are emerging alongside Digital Product Passports (DPPs) and other B2B and B2G data-sharing initiatives, notably under eIDAS2 and the EU Digital Identity Wallet for legal persons.

This document examines where VCs and Verifiable Presentations add value in Business Wallet use cases, why Business Wallets fall within the scope of the DPP & Business Wallet Task Force of the W3C Verifiable Credentials Working Group, and how far existing organisational credential formats are compatible with the VC Data Model. It is not a specification: it collects requirements, use cases and open questions to inform future standardisation of cross-border, cross-registry organisational data sharing.
